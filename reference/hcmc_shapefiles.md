# Ho Chi Minh City shapefiles data

A list of shapefiles (as `sf` objects) for Ho Chi Minh City

## Usage

``` r
hcmc_shapefiles
```

## Format

### `hcmc_shapefiles`

A named list with 5 objects

- district_24:

  24 districts in HCM city (during 2003 - 2021)

- commune_322:

  322 communes in HCM city (during 2003 - 2021)

- commune_312:

  312 communes in HCM city (during 2021 - 2023)

- commune_168:

  168 communes in HCM city (after 2025 reform)

- commune_167_mainland:

  Same as `commune_168` but only mainland communes (no Con Dao island)
